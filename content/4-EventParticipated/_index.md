This section lists and summarizes the events I have participated in. For example:

### [Event 1 - Talkshow "The Thinking Behind Building The Bot"](4.1-Event1/)
A talkshow on AI Agents and AI-powered applications, focusing on how developers think about real-world problems before building an AI bot. Speakers came from FCAJ and CMC Global. The event helped me shift my approach from simply "using AI" to designing a complete AI solution around a specific problem.

### [Event 2 - AWS Cloud Day and AI Day Hanoi - Watch Party](4.2-Event2/)
A one-day AWS event covering Agentic AI, data analytics, cloud migration, modern application development, and industry solutions. The event reinforced the idea that cloud and AI should be applied to real business problems, starting with the problem rather than the technology.

### [Event 3 - Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner](4.3-Event3/)
A community session with Dr. Werner Vogels, CTO of Amazon, on cloud computing, AI, innovation, and building systems at global scale. The session encouraged me to think about how individual AWS services work together in a complete architecture, and to consider scalability from the beginning.

### [Event 4 - FCAJ Community Day](4.4-Event4/)
A community day on modern cloud architecture, covering software factories, serverless computing with AWS Lambda, asynchronous systems with Amazon SQS, and career growth through internships and open-source contributions. The event gave me concrete ideas for optimizing my FCAJ project's architecture.
