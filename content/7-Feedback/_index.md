> Here I share my personal opinions about the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program, to help the team improve in the following areas.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working environment**
The environment is friendly and open. Members are willing to help when I get stuck, and the team makes it easy to ask questions.

**2. Support from mentor and team**
The mentor explains things clearly and encourages me to ask questions. I especially value that the mentor lets me work through problems myself before giving the answer.

**3. Relevance to my major**
The tasks connect well with what I learned at university, and they also introduced me to areas I had not worked with before, such as AWS architecture and AI applications.

**4. Learning and skill development**
I learned AWS services in practice, how to design and document a project, and how to report progress clearly. Attending events and workshops also helped me understand where the industry is heading.

**5. Team spirit**
The team works seriously and supports each other. I felt like a real member of the team, not just an intern.

**6. Program structure**
The weekly worklogs, workshops, and events give a clear path to follow, which helps me track my progress.

---

### Additional Questions

- **Most satisfying part:** Building a real project from design to deployment, and seeing it work.
- **What the program could improve:** Earlier access to the AWS environment and more time for hands-on practice.
- **Would I recommend it to a friend?** Yes. It is a good place to learn cloud skills through real work, especially for students who want practical experience.

---

### Suggestions and Expectations

- **Suggestions:** Add more hands-on labs with clear checkpoints, and share more examples from past interns' projects.
- **Continue in the future?** Yes, I would like to continue and contribute to the program.
- **Other comments:** Thank you to the mentors and the FCAJ team for their support throughout the program.
